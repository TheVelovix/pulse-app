Pulse is a mobile client for your Pulse analytics dashboard, letting you monitor your websites on the go.

Free tier includes:
* Up to 5 projects, 30 days of data retention
* Page views, unique visitors, and bounce rate
* Entry pages, top pages, and referrer tracking
* Outbound link, device, OS, and browser tracking
* AI-based traffic source detection

A paid Pro plan is available for advanced features such as unlimited projects, longer data retention, CSV export, real-time visitor counts, Google Search Console and Google Analytics integrations, and more.
